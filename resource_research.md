# Free resource research notes

- MIT OpenCourseWare official search: https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?q=computer+science
  - The official page identifies itself as MIT OpenCourseWare and describes the service as a place to explore free online course materials.
- Search results also surfaced official or primary resource families to verify and use selectively: Harvard CS50 (https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/), Cisco Networking Academy cybersecurity (https://www.netacad.com/courses/introduction-to-cybersecurity), freeCodeCamp (https://www.freecodecamp.org/learn/), Khan Academy statistics/probability (https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability), and official freeCodeCamp YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/c/Freecodecamp).
- Resource policy for Hana: prefer no-payment/no-credit-card resources; use Coursera or edX only when the specific page clearly offers free audit access; label each link by what skill it helps study and include a backup link when practical.
