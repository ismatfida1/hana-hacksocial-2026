# External source notes

## FCIT/PUCIT official BS Information Technology page

Source: https://pucit.edu.pk/bs-information-technology/

Retrieved 2026-08-25. The page is on the official PUCIT domain and identifies itself as BS (Information Technology). It links to an official Approved Curriculum page at https://pucit.edu.pk/approved-curriculum/ and includes a scheme of studies for the Fall 2021 batch onward. The page states degree objectives and shows semester-level course-category counts, but this source should be treated as an official reference link rather than used to invent a learner’s individual subjects. HANA should let the student enter or correct their own subjects when the official course list is not sufficient for their programme or batch.
